# LittleRoute asset sources

Asset sources and generation history, updated 28 October 2026. The final placement index below identifies the files currently distributed; retired generation prompts are retained as history. Stock and generated scenes illustrate the design concept; they are not photographs of an operated LittleRoute fleet or enrolled pupils. Old unused yellow-bus imagery and films have been removed.

## Generated brand and transport photography

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The PNG logo retains transparency. The five new photographs are optimised WebP copies; original generated artwork is preserved separately from the project.

- `dist/assets/littleroute-logo.png`: navy/sky-blue emblem integrating a modern minibus, passengers, an open book and a route curve. Original prompt: “A single premium logo emblem for LittleRoute, a modern school bus and student transport brand; a simplified modern minibus silhouette integrated with a protective route curve and school-book roof shape; navy #142B4A and sky blue #3282B8; transparent negative space; clean geometric shapes; no words, no yellow, no mockup.”

## Fonts and project artwork

Manrope and DM Sans are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License. Original projects: https://github.com/sharanda/manrope and https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts . See `dist/assets/OFL-Manrope.txt` and `dist/assets/OFL-DMSans.txt`.
